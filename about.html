WHO WE ARE

Built On Fabric, Print & Identity

MarkxCollections is a modern clothing and custom printing brand focused on helping people and organizations express their identity through quality T-shirts and personalized designs. What started as a small print shop has grown into a full apparel studio serving individuals, students and companies across Pakistan.

We work across four lanes: everyday fashion, custom DTF printing, single-piece orders, and large bulk runs for schools, teams and companies — all from one place.
